How scoring works

Every claim names a metric, a comparison, a threshold and a resolution date (tap rule on any claim to see it). On that date it is scored against the archived snapshot for that day — not a fresh lookup.

The honest floor is a 95% Wilson lower bound. A good hit rate over a handful of claims means very little, and the floor is the number worth trusting until the sample is large. A claim that cannot be resolved is marked void rather than quietly dropped, and the reason is shown with it.

Every prediction is also posted as the first comment under its episode. Reply YES or NO before the vote closes (48 hours after publishing) and your call is counted: one vote per account, the latest one counts, and the majority is scored against the same measurement as ours. A tie is no call.
