Every prediction is posted as the first comment under its episode. Reply YES or NO before the vote closes (48 hours after publishing) and your call is counted: one vote per account, the latest one counts, and the majority is scored against the same measurement as ours. A tie is no call.

The honest floor is a 95% Wilson lower bound. A good hit rate over a handful of claims means very little, and the floor is the number worth trusting until the sample is large. A claim that cannot be resolved — because the pipeline did not run that day — is marked void rather than quietly dropped.
