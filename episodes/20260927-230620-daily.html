Cold open

6.81 million dollars changed hands on Strike Finance, Cardano's perpetuals venue, in the last day, across 30 markets. Here is how its prices hold up against the biggest exchanges.

Cardano's perps venue, measured

A perpetual contract lets you bet on a price going up or down without owning the thing itself, and with no expiry date. Strike Finance, Cardano's perpetuals venue, traded 6.81 million dollars across 30 markets in the last 24 hours. ADA was the busiest: 2.21 million dollars, 32 percent of the total. Its prices track the big exchanges closely: across the majors, the median gap to the same contract on OKX was 4.4 hundredths of a percent. The quoted spread on ADA was about 4 hundredths of a percent. And a resting order that got filled was, five minutes later, 0.1 hundredths of a percent behind on average — which is what it costs to stand in front of faster traders. A market's volume and the gap to other venues say whether it is a real place to trade, which a funding rate alone cannot. A funding rate is what one side of a perpetual pays the other to keep its position open. Why it matters: volume and a tight gap to the big venues are what make a market real, not the number of contracts listed.

ADA

Also today. ADA is at 25.0 cents, down 0.6 percent on the day — up 24.6 percent over thirty days and up 7.9 percent on the week. Since the last episode it has moved down 1.2 percent.

Following the treasury's money

One more thing. Since Cardano's on-chain governance began paying out, 78 treasury withdrawals have been enacted, sending 804.4 million ADA to 17 recipients. 15 of those recipients are smart contracts — treasury vaults that hold the money and release it under their own rules — and they received 752.1 million ADA. Of that, 39.3 million ADA, about 5 percent, is still inside those vaults today. The other 95 percent has moved out. Moved out means the ADA left the vault. The chain does not say what it was used for — that part is up to the recipients to report. It is the first step in following public money from a vote to the work it was approved for.
