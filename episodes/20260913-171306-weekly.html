Measuring the 44% buffer in Cardano's on-chain loan collateral ratios

Watch on YouTube · the figures on screen (JSON) · every claim and how it resolved

Transcript

Cold open

Cardano's native tokens carry 762.89 million dollars of market value on paper. The amount that could actually be sold before moving the price ten percent is 369.0 thousand dollars — 0.05 percent of it. Here is the week, measured.

Paper value vs exit value

Market cap is price times supply, but that price only holds for the next small trade. Across Cardano's native tokens the paper figure is 762.89 million dollars. The amount that could actually be sold before moving the price ten percent is 369.0 thousand dollars. That is 0.05 percent of the stated value. That covers 48 tokens whose decimals and pool depth could both be verified on chain; 73 more were excluded because their supply figures are not trustworthy. These market caps are upper bounds — no treasury or vesting exclusions are applied unless a token's float could be verified, and the table says which is which.

What moved

Against the last episode on 2026-09-13: ADA down 1.4 percent, Indigo TVL flat 0.0 percent, Liquidation floor down 1.4 percent. The liquidation floor is the one to watch — it moves when borrowers add or remove collateral, not when the price does.

Indigo TVL, all of it

Zooming out past the daily noise. Indigo's total value locked is 4.03 million dollars today, against an all-time peak of 125.56 million dollars in March 2024. That is 96.8 percent below the high. Over the past year TVL is down 89.5 percent, and over ninety days up 9.5 percent. This is nearly four years of daily history, and the trend matters more than any single day in it.

The liquidation floor, over time

One number worth tracking episode to episode is the price at which half the loan book would be at risk. Across the last 5 days it has risen 0.5 percent, from 11.4 cents to 11.5 cents. This moves when borrowers add or remove collateral, not when the ADA price moves — so it reads on what borrowers are doing, not on the market.

The loan book

Indigo's borrowers have 3.83 million dollars of ADA locked against 1.76 million dollars of debt, across 290 live positions. That is a book-wide collateral ratio of 2.17 to one. These figures are decoded from the chain directly, position by position.

How far can ADA fall?

Here is the part nobody publishes. Ten percent of that debt enters the liquidation band once ADA reaches 15.2 cents — a drop of 26.0 percent from here. Half the book needs ADA at 11.5 cents, which is 44.0 percent below today. So the honest read is that this loan book is built for a deep drawdown, not a shallow one.

Who holds it

Underneath the prices, the holder distributions. NIGHT is held by 60.9 thousand addresses. Its Nakamoto coefficient is 10 — that is how many holders would need to act together to control a majority of supply. The top ten addresses hold 52.2 percent of it. These are aggregate figures from the chain; no individual wallet is identified here.

What passed

Over the last ninety days 12 actions reached an outcome: 3 enacted, 9 expired without passing. Behind those votes sit 870 registered DReps holding 15.22 billion ADA, with the five largest holding 74.6 percent of it.

Treasury outflow

Governance moves money, not just opinions. Over the last 180 days 389.6 million ADA has been withdrawn from the Cardano treasury and enacted — about 79.96 million dollars at today's price — across 26 separate withdrawals. Lifetime, 804.4 million ADA has left the treasury. That is new supply arriving in circulation, and it is worth watching alongside the price.

The base layer

Stepping back to the base layer. The last complete epoch settled 108.5 thousand transactions and collected 32.6 thousand ADA in fees. That is up 1.3 percent on the epoch before it. 21.37 billion ADA is delegated across 2,675 active stake pools, which is 47.5 percent of the 45 billion maximum supply.

Where the rewards come from

Staking rewards are not created from nothing. They are paid out of Cardano's reserve, which now holds 6.13 billion ADA. That reserve is drawn down by a fixed fraction every epoch — measured over the last 39 epochs, 0.162 percent per epoch, or about 10.3 million ADA. At that rate the reserve halves roughly every 5.9 years. It does not run out on a date, it decays, and as it shrinks a larger share of rewards has to come from transaction fees instead. The treasury meanwhile accrues about 3.7 million ADA per epoch, and paid out 392.2 million ADA across 12 enacted withdrawals in the same window.

Cross-check

Every number in this segment gets checked against a second source before it airs. Debt decoded from individual positions is compared with total supply minted on chain. 6 of 7 assets reconcile at exactly one hundred percent. iUSD does not — it comes in at 92.11 percent, and that gap is disclosed rather than rounded away.
