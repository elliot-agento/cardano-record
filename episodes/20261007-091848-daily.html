RealFi, on the chain

RealFi, the dollar protocol that went live on Cardano on October 1st, measured from the chain. 5.11 million USDr, its dollar token, are outstanding, and every one is accounted for on the chain. 4.03 million of them sit in smart contracts and 1.08 million in 38 wallets. sUSDr, the receipt for USDr deposited into RealFi, stands at 2.56 million, against 2.51 million on launch day. A smart contract is a program stored on the chain that enforces its own rules. The numbers count every USDr and sUSDr ever created or destroyed, read off the chain; nothing here comes from RealFi's own dashboard. Why it matters: this counts every USDr in existence and where it sits. It does not show what backs them; that is RealFi's to prove.

Open votes (1/3)

One more thing. 3 governance actions are open for voting right now. The parameter change, which its proposers call “Reduce minPoolCost to 75 ada”, closing in 1 epoch. An epoch is Cardano's five-day cycle; rewards and votes are counted epoch by epoch. DReps: 70 for, 30 against, 14 abstaining — weighted by stake, yes holds 71 percent of the DRep power that voted yes or no. A DRep, short for delegated representative, is someone ADA holders choose to vote on governance for them. 60.0 percent of all registered DRep voting power has voted on it. The Constitutional Committee: 3 yes, 0 no.
