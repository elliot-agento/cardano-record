Cardano (ADA) News Today: 1.5M ADA idle for over three years moved in a day

Nov 25 · daily

Watch on YouTube · the figures on screen (JSON) · every claim and how it resolved

Transcript

Cold open

1.5 million ADA that had not moved in more than three years moved in the last day. Here is how long all the ADA that moved had been sitting still.

Last week's calls

First, the scorecard. We said 100 MIN tokens are worth more than 1.558 ADA on November 25, a bar set 9.5 percent below where it was on November 18. It measured 1.682 ADA, so we got that one right. That puts the running record at 23 right, 16 wrong, 1 void.

Old coins on the move

How long had the ADA that moved today been sitting still? Of the 6.60 billion ADA in outputs spent over the last 24 hours, 98.2 percent had existed for under thirty days: the same coins circulating. 1.5 million ADA had sat untouched for more than three years before it moved, across 1,017 separate outputs. That is about 2 in every 10,000 of the ADA that has sat that long. By our own count, that is inside the range of the 51 earlier days we have recorded, 1 million to 23.1 million. That 6.60 billion counts a coin every time it is spent, so the same coins are counted many times over; about 13 percent of today's total came from a single keyholder. Moving is not selling. Coins shuffled between one holder's own addresses count here too.

All the ADA, by how long it has sat

Now step back from one day to all of it. As of Saturday's full count, of the 37.14 billion ADA sitting in wallets and contracts, 7.16 billion, 19.3 percent, has not moved in more than three years. Only 16.5 percent has moved in the last thirty days. That count takes each coin once, which is why it is smaller than the single day of spending, where busy coins are counted again and again. Long-unmoved ADA includes lost keys and exchange cold storage as well as patient holders; the chain cannot tell them apart, and this does not try. Why it matters: how long coins sit before they move separates the same coins circulating from holdings that had been left alone for years.

ADA

Also today. ADA is at 13.9 cents, down 4.2 percent on the day — down 31.4 percent over thirty days and down 8.8 percent on the week. Since the last episode it has moved down 7.2 percent. That is 3.4 percent above the bottom of its thirty-day range, 13.5 cents.

Who is building: Liqwid

One more thing. Who is building Cardano. Today: Liqwid, a lending app, and the team behind it, Liqwid Labs. Liqwid lets people borrow against their ADA without selling it. A borrower locks ADA in the app as security, called collateral, and borrows another token against it. If ADA's price falls so far that the collateral no longer covers the loan, anyone can repay that loan and take the collateral, according to Liqwid's docs. Liqwid Labs launched it on Cardano in February 2023, according to Liqwid's own blog. Why it matters: a loan secured by ADA is a basic money tool, and here its rules run on Cardano itself, where each loan and its collateral can be looked up. This isn't a recommendation to use it; like any app that holds funds, it carries risk.
