Wallets using Cardano

How many wallets actually use Cardano? Over the past week, 5.5 thousand wallets a day sent at least one transaction, 3 percent more than the week before. Over thirty days, 90 thousand different wallets sent something, 16 percent fewer than the thirty days before. Of the wallets active in the thirty days before that, 21 percent came back. A wallet here is a stake key. One person can hold several, and an exchange can hold thousands of customers behind one, so this counts wallets, not people. Why it matters: wallets that send are the closest the chain gets to counting who actually uses it.

ADA

Also today. ADA is at 17.0 cents, down 1.6 percent on the day — down 27.6 percent over thirty days and down 7.2 percent on the week. Since the last episode it has moved down 2.8 percent. That is 1.6 percent above the bottom of its thirty-day range, 16.7 cents.

Closest to the line

One more thing. The book as a whole is one number; the CDPs inside it are not all in the same place. These are the positions sitting closest to the line. The nearest one holds 30.3 thousand dollars of debt against 207,923 ADA, and is already inside the liquidation band at today's price. Liquidation means a position is closed automatically because its collateral no longer covers what it owes. 5 positions carry enough debt to matter here. 3 smaller positions, each under 1,000 dollars of debt, hold 745.26 dollars between them and are left out.
