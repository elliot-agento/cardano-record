What crossed the bridge

Assets that came from another chain are the one part of Cardano where you can see value actually crossing a boundary, because a bridged token only exists here while something is held somewhere else. Over 7 days, 6 of the 19 assets from the 3 mint-and-burn bridges we track saw any mint or burn. The other 13 had none at all. Rosen's rsERG is left out of those counts: it was last minted in 2023, and the bridge pays out of a reserve made up front, so mints and burns cannot show how much crosses. The largest move was BTC, whose outstanding amount on Cardano shrank 12.50 percent across 1 mint and 20 burns. 2 grew and 4 shrank, so this was not a week with one direction to it. Two things this deliberately does not do. It does not put these in dollars, because the decimal places on a bridged token are not something we have verified — and it does not tell you the collateral is really locked on the other side, because that is not visible from Cardano. What the ledger settles is how much is represented here, and that is what you just heard. Why it matters: a bridged token only exists here while something is held on the other chain, so its supply is a direct reading of value crossing over.

ADA

Also today. ADA is at 15.3 cents, up 2.9 percent on the day — down 26.4 percent over thirty days and up 2.5 percent on the week. Since the last episode it has moved up 9.6 percent.

Prediction markets

One more thing. Prediction markets on Cardano. Bodega runs yes-or-no markets settled on chain, and according to an independent indexer's decode of its contracts, 9 are open right now, holding 19.1 thousand ADA. 2,600 ADA has changed hands in them so far. Results are written on the chain by the Bodega team itself. No independent oracle confirms them. An oracle is a service that writes outside data, such as prices, onto the chain. It is a small market today, and this is its size.
