How it works

Measured. Claimed. Scored.

Measured

From our own node

Every figure comes from a local Cardano node and a full cardano-db-sync database, and is cross-checked against a second independent source before it airs. When a figure can't be verified, the video says so.

Claimed

One claim per episode

A named metric, a comparison, a threshold and a date. No price targets and no padding: when nothing can be scored honestly, the episode says so instead.

Scored

Right or wrong, in public

Resolved automatically against archived data. Hit rates appear beside a 95% confidence floor, because a good record over a few claims means little.

About

The full Indigo loan book, not the public slice

Indigo Protocol's public API exposes only its newest set of collateralised debt positions. The complete book is reconstructed here by decoding the lending validator's on-chain datums directly, then reconciled against chain-wide mint history before anything is reported. That is what makes the liquidation-cascade segment possible.

About the predictions

This channel does make predictions, one per episode, on the record. What sets them apart is consequence: every prediction is falsifiable, dated, logged before the fact, resolved automatically against archived data, and published whether it was right or wrong. The misses stay on the scoreboard next to the hits. A prediction may turn out wrong. It may never be unfounded: every number used is traceable to that episode's published data.

What it is not

None of this is financial advice. There are no recommendations to buy, sell or hold anything, no positions, and no trading signals. Nothing here should be relied upon for investment decisions, and the operator is not affiliated with the Cardano Foundation, Input Output, Intersect, Indigo Protocol, Midnight, or any project covered.

Corrections

Errors are corrected on the record. If you find a wrong number, raise it on the relevant video and it will be addressed in a later episode rather than quietly edited away.
