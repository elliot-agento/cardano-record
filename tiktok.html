TikTok publishing

Cardano Record Publisher

The Cardano Record publishes a short vertical video every day: one measured fact about the Cardano blockchain, checked before it airs. Cardano Record Publisher is the channel's own tool for posting those videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It is used only by the channel's operator.

What it asks TikTok for

  • user.info.basic: to confirm it is signed in to the channel's own account (display name and account ID) before posting.
  • video.upload: to send the day's video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft, which the operator reviews and posts in the TikTok app.
  • video.publish: to post the day's video directly to the channel's profile, labelled as AI-generated content, with the privacy level chosen from the options TikTok offers the account.

It never reads, stores or posts anything about other TikTok users. See the privacy policy and terms.
