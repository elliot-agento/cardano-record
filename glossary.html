Glossary

Cardano terms in plain words

The words our episodes use, each explained in one sentence.

collateral

Collateral is what someone locks up to back what they owe or have minted; if its value stops covering that, it goes to whoever pays off the debt.

decimals

A token's decimals say where the decimal point goes in its on-chain amounts; get them wrong and every price is off by a factor of ten or more.

DEX

A DEX, or decentralised exchange, lets people swap tokens straight from their wallets.

DRep

A DRep, short for delegated representative, is someone ADA holders choose to vote on governance for them.

epoch

An epoch is Cardano's five-day cycle; rewards and votes are counted epoch by epoch.

float

A token's float is the part of its supply that is actually in circulation, not locked up or held back.

funding rate

A funding rate is what one side of a perpetual pays the other to keep its position open.

governance action

A governance action is a formal proposal written on-chain and put to a vote.

liquidation

Liquidation means a position is closed automatically because its collateral no longer covers what it owes.

liquidity pool

A liquidity pool is a pot of two tokens that traders swap against; the people who fill it earn the trading fees.

NFT

An NFT is a token minted as one of a kind.

oracle

An oracle is a service that writes outside data, such as prices, onto the chain.

output

An output is one chunk of ADA that a transaction leaves at an address; the next transaction to spend it uses it up whole.

perpetual

A perpetual is a contract that tracks a price with no expiry date.

pool depth

Pool depth is how much is sitting in a token's trading pools, which sets how much can be sold before the price drops.

protocol parameter

A protocol parameter is one of Cardano's adjustable settings, changed by governance vote.

reserve

The reserve is the ADA not yet released into circulation, and it pays most staking rewards.

smart contract

A smart contract is a program stored on the chain that enforces its own rules.

stablecoin

A stablecoin is a token built to stay worth one dollar.

stake pool

A stake pool is a node that produces blocks on behalf of the ADA delegated to it.

TVL

TVL, or total value locked, is the dollar value of everything deposited in a protocol.

utilisation

Utilisation is the share of a lending pool that has been borrowed.

UTxO

A UTxO, or unspent transaction output, is one separate chunk of ADA or tokens sitting at an address.

wrapped token

A wrapped token stands for a coin locked on another chain.
