Can you beat Ellie?

Forecast every prediction from the show. Scored automatically against the same on-chain data, right next to Ellie's record.

How it works

Every daily episode ends with a prediction: a number, a threshold and a date, scored automatically from archived on-chain data. Here you can make your own call on each one: how likely is it to come true?

Forecasting closes halfway to the resolution date, and you can change your mind until then. The crowd's average stays hidden until forecasting closes, so nobody copies it.

Scoring uses the Brier score: the square of the gap between your forecast and what happened. Saying 90% on something that comes true scores 0.01; saying 90% on something that doesn't scores 0.81. Lower is better, so confident and right beats cautious, and confident and wrong costs the most.

You vs. Ellie. The headline is right calls: a forecast over 50% counts as a yes, under 50% as a no, and Ellie's call is always yes, because each claim is her prediction. Ellie also has a number for each prediction: the chance her claim-picking model gave it when the claim was made, logged before the outcome. It shows here only after forecasting closes, so nobody can copy it, and it is capped at 5–95% and scored with Brier exactly like yours.

Free to play. No money, no prizes, no tokens. Your handle is public; your email and wallet never are. Wallet sign-in only signs a message. It can never move or spend anything.
