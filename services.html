How we work

Built on our own node. A local Cardano node and a full db-sync database, with decoders for protocols whose own APIs show only part of the picture — Indigo's public API, for example, lists a small fraction of its CDP book.

Checked before it ships. Figures are reconciled against a second source, stale inputs are dropped rather than shown, and every claim the channel makes is scored in public on the scoreboard.

Sponsorship rules. A sponsored segment is labelled on screen and in the description. Sponsors never choose, review or change what is reported, and no number is for sale.
